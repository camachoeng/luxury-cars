2. Services

YMV Limo provides premium intercity and hourly chauffeur services in the greater Houston area and across Texas. All bookings are subject to vehicle and driver availability. We reserve the right to assign any suitable vehicle from our fleet.

3. Booking & Payment

A valid credit or debit card is required to complete a booking. Your card is saved securely via Stripe and will be charged after your trip is completed or per the cancellation policy below. The fare shown at booking is an estimate — the final amount is confirmed by the admin after vehicle assignment.

4. Cancellation Policy

More than 6 hours before pickup: $20 flat admin fee.

Within 6 hours of pickup: 50% of the estimated fare.

No-show: Full fare charged. Our driver will wait up to 30 minutes past the scheduled pickup time before the booking is marked as a no-show.

Cancellations must be made through the My Bookings page. Cancellations requested via WhatsApp or phone are not accepted and will not be processed.
